Travel Experience & Roadmap

From your doorstep in Salem to the misty heights of Yercaud and back — step by step.

Phase 01 • Morning

Start Your Journey

Punctual pickup at your doorstep in Salem (between 7:00 AM & 9:00 AM). Enjoy a clean, air-conditioned sedan cabin.

Phase 02 • Ghat Ascent

Reach Yercaud

Ascend through the legendary 20 hairpin bends. Feel the temperature drop and view the spreading plains below.

Phase 03 • Day Tour

Explore Yercaud

Visit Yercaud Lake, Lady's Seat, Pagoda Point, Botanical Garden & drive the 32-km loop road. Lunch at recommended restaurants.

Phase 04 • Evening

Return Safely

Smooth descent with evening sunset views at Lady's Seat, followed by comfortable drop-off back in Salem (6:00–8:00 PM).
