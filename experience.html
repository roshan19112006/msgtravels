Curated Hill Station Itineraries

Step-by-step travel schedules for your holiday in Yercaud, Ooty, Kodaikanal, and Munnar hill stations.

📍
07:30 AM • Pickup

Salem Doorstep Departure

Driver arrives punctually at your Salem home, hotel, or Salem Railway Junction in sanitized AC car.

⛰️
08:15 AM • Mountain Drive

20 Hairpin Bends Ascent

Smooth, safe mountain ascent with breathtaking aerial views of the Salem plains and lush greenery.

🚣
09:30 AM • Boating & Garden

Emerald Lake & Orchidarium

Pedal boating on Yercaud Lake followed by a leisurely stroll through the National Orchidarium.

🌲
01:30 PM • Scenic Circuit

32-Km Loop Road & Kiliyur Falls

Post-lunch drive through heritage British coffee estates, bamboo thickets, and Kiliyur cascade.

🌄
04:30 PM • Sunset Panorama

Lady's Seat & Pagoda Point

Watch the sun set behind the hills and view the glowing city lights before safely returning to Salem by 7:30 PM.

🚗
Day 01 • Morning

Salem ➔ Mettupalayam ➔ Ooty

Depart Salem at 6:00 AM. Ascend the Nilgiri hills through scenic tea gardens and check in by 11:30 AM.

🌸
Day 01 • Afternoon

Botanical Garden & Ooty Lake

Explore the 55-acre Government Botanical Garden, Rose Garden, and enjoy evening lake boating & cycling.

🔭
Day 02 • Morning

Doddabetta Peak (8,650 ft) & Tea Factory

360-degree Nilgiri summit telescope view, fresh tea processing demo, and homemade chocolate sampling.

🌊
Day 02 • Afternoon

Pykara Lake or Coonoor Dolphin's Nose

Speedboating at Pykara or picturesque excursion to Sim's Park and Catherine Falls view before evening return.

🚗
Day 01 • Morning

Salem ➔ Silver Cascade ➔ Kodai Lake

Scenic drive via Dindigul ghats, greeting Silver Cascade Falls on entrance and checking into Kodaikanal.

🚣
Day 01 • Afternoon

Star Lake Boating, Cycling & Bryant Park

Relaxing boating on Kodai Star Lake, bicycle hire on the 5km promenade, and Bryant Park flower glasshouse.

🌲
Day 02 • Morning

Coaker's Walk & Green Valley View

Morning walk along mountain clouds with sweeping valley views and telescope house.

🗿
Day 02 • Afternoon

Pillar Rocks, Pine Forest & Guna Caves

Towering 400 ft granite boulders, towering European pines, and photography stops before heading back.

🚗
Day 01 • Journey

Salem ➔ Marayoor ➔ Munnar Arrival

Drive through Udumalpet and Marayoor sandalwood reserves into Munnar's rolling tea velvet slopes.

🚤
Day 02 • Full Day

Mattupetty Dam, Kundala Lake & Echo Point

Speedboat ride on Mattupetty reservoir, Kashmiri Shikara boats at Kundala arch dam, and natural echo point.

🐐
Day 03 • Morning

Eravikulam National Park (Anamudi)

Spot endangered Nilgiri Tahr mountain goats and witness South India's highest peak Anamudi (8,842 ft).

🍃
Day 03 • Afternoon

Tea Museum & Scenic Return to Salem

KDHP Tea Museum heritage tour, fresh spice shopping, and comfortable return journey to Salem.
